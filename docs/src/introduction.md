# Introduction

Planning Poker is a free, anonymous, link-only tool for estimating work as a
team. There's nothing to sign up for: one person creates a room, shares the
link (or a QR code), and everyone else joins straight into the browser —
no installs, no accounts, no email addresses.

A room only exists while people are in it. Nothing is saved to a server
database — once everyone leaves, the room and everything in it (votes,
tasks, results) is gone for good. If you want to keep a record of what was
estimated, grab the [session summary](./ending-a-session.md) before you
close the tab.

![Host view — invite QR code, round controls, task management, and live results](./images/host.png)

## What you can do

- **[Create a room](./creating-a-room.md)** in a few seconds and invite
  people with a link or QR code.
- **[Vote](./voting.md)** on the task currently up for estimation, using
  Fibonacci, Modified Fibonacci, T-shirt sizes, powers of two, or your own
  custom deck.
- **[See results at a glance](./results.md)** — the average front and
  center, with the lowest, highest, and majority vote(s) tagged with who
  cast them.
- **[Queue up multiple tasks](./tasks.md)** and move through them one at a
  time.
- Give trusted teammates **[host or admin powers](./roles.md)**, or step
  back and **[spectate](./spectator-mode.md)** without leaving the room.
- **[Wrap up with a summary](./ending-a-session.md)** of every task, its
  votes, and its average — ready to copy or save.

Keep reading for a walkthrough of each part of the app, or jump to the
[FAQ](./faq.md) if something isn't behaving the way you expect.
